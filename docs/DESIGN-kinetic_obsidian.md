## Brand & Style

The design system evolves the pure monochrome baseline into a high-octane, luminous dark aesthetic. It fuses the structural discipline of Swiss modernist layouts with a futuristic cyberpunk glow. Tailored for dynamic real-time communication, technical discovery, and interactive intelligence, it swaps clinical greyscale for immersive, chromatic dimensionality without sacrificing professional restraint.

### Target Audience & Persona
Built for next-generation technologists, digital creators, power users, and real-time community curators who desire an electrifying, ultra-responsive workspace that feels both surgically precise and visually alive.

### Aesthetic Tone
- **Luminous Cybernetic Accents:** Vivid violet and sharp cyan lights cut through rich indigo-tinted obsidian grounds.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep indigo-slate containers, low-frequency atmospheric glows, and translucent glass tiers replace flat, stark cutoffs.
- **High-Velocity Rhythm:** Confident geometric shapes, razor-thin luminous hairline dividers, and deliberate micro-gradient transitions that convey speed and forward momentum.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture departs from stark desaturated neutrals, employing an indigo-tinted dark foundation (`#0B0C16`) that intensifies chromatic accents.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#8B5CF6`):** Neon Violet anchors high-priority actions, selected navigational items, progress indicators, and primary visual emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4`):** Cyber Cyan provides sharp contrast for live system indicators, interactive tags, active links, and secondary metric visualizations.
- **Tertiary (`#F97316`):** Energetic Coral/Amber serves as a high-heat highlight channel for notifications, time-sensitive badges, alerts, and live pulses.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0B0C16`):** A deep nocturnal indigo bedrock, eliminating cold neutral gray to produce high-dynamic-range color rendering on OLED screens.

### Palette Mechanics
1. **Luminous Hierarchy:** Foreground text shifts from crisp white-slate (`#F8FAFC`) to muted technical steel (`#94A3B8`).
2. **Chromatic Outlines:** Component borders utilize subtle violet or cyan tinting (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)` or `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`) rather than dead gray hairlines.
3. **Multi-Stop Energy Gradients:** Premium call-to-actions, active indicators, and hero highlights blend `neon-violet` into `cyber-cyan` or `electric-purple` at a 135-degree vector.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes Inter, balancing modern Swiss mechanical discipline with micro-tuned legibility under dark mode illumination.

### Hierarchical Principles
- **Headlines & Displays:** Tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` down to `-0.01em`) provides dense, commanding visual weight that contrasts against luminous background tones.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy preserves optical breathing space with balanced `1.4` to `1.5` line-heights, ensuring optimal scanning of dense threads and text inputs.
- **Technical Readouts:** `label-technical` is deployed for metadata chips, environment badges (`DEMO · APP DE PRUEBA`), status flags, and timestamps. It enforces uppercase typesetting with `+0.08em` tracking and tabular numerals (`tnum`) to eliminate width flutter during streaming updates.

## Layout & Spacing

A modular 4px/8px layout system maintains precise geometric pacing while scaling flexibly across dense handheld screens and wide desktop viewports.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Fluid Structural Canvas:** Feed items, compose drawers, and timeline sequences span full viewport width on mobile devices, partitioned with 1px colored hairline dividers. 
- **Adaptive Breakpoints:**
  - *Mobile (< 640px):* Single continuous stream, edge-to-edge content containers, 12px–16px safe margins, and fixed bottom navigation docked above safe-area insets.
  - *Tablet & Desktop (≥ 640px):* Structural split-grid featuring a fixed navigation rail (72px to 240px), centered feed column bounded at 620px, and contextual utility widgets bounded at 340px.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Gaps and internal paddings are strictly mapped to the `space-*` scale to ensure symmetrical alignment between user avatars, content blocks, and action groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons flat gray planes, deploying stacked tonal indigos, frosted glass transradiancy, and subtle neon back-glows.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Nocturnal Bedrock):** Base canvas `#0B0C16`.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#121422` with a 1px border of `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Sheets & Popovers):** `#1A1D30` augmented with a dual-stage shadow: `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)` layered with a subtle chromatic aura `0 0 16px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`.
- **Layer 3 (Navigation & App Bars):** Translucent indigo surface (`rgba(11, 12, 22, 0.75)`) infused with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and grounded by a bottom border of `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.18)`.
- **Interactive Luminescence:** Primary focus rings and active element states cast a tight, vibrant 3px glow (`0 0 12px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system establishes a high-precision rounded geometric identity that pairs smooth structural containers with energetic interactive pills.

### Geometry Breakdown
- **Standard Structural Elements:** Cards, input wrappers, modal sheets, and dialogue panels leverage standard rounding (`0.5rem` / 8px) up to `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for elevated surface cards.
- **Interactive Targets:** Primary action triggers, filter chips, active indicators, and profile tags adopt pill rounding (`9999px`), reinforcing clear separation between static containers and clickable elements.
- **Media & Avatar Rings:** Media thumbnails match the card curvature (`0.5rem`), while user avatars remain strictly circular with 1.5px concentric accent rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Dynamic linear gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #8B5CF6 0%, #06B6D4 100%)`), text `#F8FAFC`, font weight `600`, pill radius (`9999px`). Hover triggers a luminous bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.45)`). Pressed state scales to `0.98`.
- **Secondary Action:** `#1A1D30` background, border `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`, text `#06B6D4`. Hover fills with `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`.
- **Ghost / Icon:** Transparent ground, icon color `#94A3B8`. Hover shifts icon to `#8B5CF6` with a circular background of `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Technical Badge ('DEMO · APP DE PRUEBA'):** Background `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`, text `#8B5CF6`, rounded `0.25rem`. Renders `label-technical` type.
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive in `#121422` with border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.16)`. Active state utilizes a subtle cyan gradient background with `1px solid #06B6D4` and crisp `#F8FAFC` typography.

### Input Fields
- **Fields & Search Bars:** Background `#121422`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#8B5CF6` backed by an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`).

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unselected outline in `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.35)`. Checked state transitions into a solid `#8B5CF6` fill with an indigo checkmark, complemented by a cyan pulse ring on focus.
- **Switches:** Track shifts from `#1A1D30` to a vibrant cyan-violet gradient when toggled on, accompanied by a glowing white thumb.

### Cards & Timeline Feed Items
- **Feed Item Row:** Bounded by a bottom border of `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)`. Hover produces an ambient wash of `rgba(18, 20, 34, 0.6)`.
- **Metric Controls:** Counter icons default to `#94A3B8`. Interactive states trigger contextual chromatic shifts: Violet for Repost, Cyan for Reply, Coral for Like/Favorite.
